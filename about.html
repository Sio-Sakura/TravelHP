The New Englander

Brian

Boston, MA → North Carolina → New England

Brian grew up in a suburb of Boston, MA. Growing up, his family traveled all over New England and to places like Williamsburg, VA, New York City, Orlando, FL, and Washington, D.C.

In High School, Brian traveled to Italy for 2 weeks with a choir group from the New England Conservatory in Boston. Since then, he has visited places like the UK, Canada, and Japan.

From 2007 – 2020, he lived in North Carolina before returning to New England, where he currently resides.

Where we’re headed next

These are the trips at the top of our list right now. We look overseas as well as around the US, choose activities to fit the season, and travel by train and car as well as by plane. Here is what we are planning next.

New York → Las Vegas

Across America by Train

We want to cross the country by rail, from New York to Las Vegas. The plan is to book a sleeper car and watch the Colorado Rockies and the red canyons of Utah roll past the window.

Europe

Ireland

Ireland is at the top of our Europe list. We are picturing green coastal cliffs, old stone castles, and evenings of live music in a small-town pub.

New England · Winter

A Snowy Cabin Stay

We are looking for an Airbnb in New England with a fireplace and snow piling up outside. During the day we want to try winter sports, starting with snowmobiling.

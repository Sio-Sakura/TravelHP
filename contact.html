    Quick answers to the usual questions

    Browse all guides

    Can you plan our trip for us?

    We don’t offer custom planning, but our guides are built to be copied. Start with the destination guide, then the tips section.

    Do you accept free stays or products?

    Sometimes — always disclosed, never in exchange for a positive review. We only feature places we’d return to on our own dime.

    How long until you reply?

    Usually 2–3 days. When we’re off-grid in the mountains it can take a little longer — we’ll always get back to you.

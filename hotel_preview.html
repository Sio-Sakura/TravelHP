The Best Hotel Near Takasaki Station: A Review of Hotel Coco Grand

The best hotel near Takasaki Station is the Hotel Coco Grand Takasaki.

It’s just a couple of minutes’ walk from the station itself, and as of writing this in May of 2026, it’s a newer hotel and the quality is really high. I almost feel like they took the lessons learned from older hotels and made a lot of improvements when building this one. When I stay here, everything is just one step (or more) above other hotels I’ve stayed at in the area.

  • Key Takeaway: Hotel Coco Grand Takasaki is a newer, higher-quality property within a couple minutes’ walk of Takasaki Station — and after six-plus months of total stays, it consistently outperforms other hotels in the area on the details that actually matter.

I’ve stayed here a lot. I’ve been coming to Japan for years, and when I do, I work while I’m here. So during the week, I’m at the Coco Grand. I’ve stayed here for probably a total of at least six months, so I’ve had plenty of time to experience the hotel and think about this review.

Why the Coco Grand is the Best Hotel Near Takasaki Station

Here are the reasons this hotel stands above the rest in the area:

Booking Online is Easy

Booking on the website is extremely easy. I don’t speak Japanese, so I have to translate the website to English, and usually translations aren’t as good as the native language. But even with this deficit, it only took me a couple of minutes to figure things out, and now booking is a breeze.

The bottom line: That’s your first interaction with the hotel, so it’s nice to have it be a pleasant one.

The Staff is Kind and Helpful

The staff are incredibly kind and helpful. They don’t all speak English, but some do, and their English is quite good. Even when I’ve encountered staff that don’t speak English (and to be clear, I don’t speak fluent Japanese either), checking in has been incredibly simple.

They are kind, patient, and consistent. It won’t be long until you have your room keys and you’re ready to open the door and relax.

The Lobby is Beautiful

The lobby is beautiful, and it’s great for relaxing or working. Sometimes I even prefer it over cafes.

  • Water everywhere — the sight and sound of it is incredibly calming
  • Nice wood tables — comfortable chairs and benches throughout
  • A bar off to the side — at night it’s a really nice environment with people drinking, talking, and relaxing

You can sit and sip a drink of your own or work on your computer — either way, it works.

The Elevators are Fast

The elevators are fast. I know this may seem like a small thing to some, but if you’re experienced staying in hotels, you know it’s the little things that get to you after a while. In a lot of other hotels, the elevators can take quite a long time. But at the Coco Grand, the elevators arrive quickly after you press the button, and they get you to your floor quickly as well.

  • Pet Peeve Avoided: Some hotels have elevators that automatically stop at the lobby even if you didn’t press that button — trying to go to floor one and the lobby is on floor two? You’re stopping anyway. It’s a nuisance, and it does not happen at the Coco Grand.

Quiet Floors and Spacious Rooms

The floors are quiet and the rooms are spacious. I’ve never had a problem with noise. It’s very private, and the rooms are genuinely roomy. I’ve been in the smallest room they have multiple times, and it’s far bigger than what you get at a lot of other hotels. And of course, if you book one of the bigger rooms, you’ll have quite a lot of space.

Pro tip: The showers, especially the ones behind the glass doors, are amazing. Not all the rooms have these, but if you’re able to book one with a glass shower enclosure, it’s a shower you are not going to want to leave.

The Wi-Fi is Strong, Fast, and Consistent

This is the best Wi-Fi out of any hotel I’ve tried in the Takasaki Station area. It’s strong, fast, and consistent. That is absolutely not the case at other hotels around here.

Like I said, when I visit and stay at this hotel, I’m working, and I need the connection to be reliable. Every time at the Coco Grand, it is.

The Beds are Incredibly Comfortable

The pillows, blankets, and mattresses are the most comfortable of any hotel I’ve been to.

They Have Massage Chairs

Yes, the Coco Grand has massage chairs! This has to be uncommon because I have not seen it in any other hotels I’ve stayed at in the Takasaki station area.

The first time I stayed here, it was with my wife (she’s Japanese), and the first thing she did was hop into that chair. Neck rubs, shoulder rubs, calf massage – you can get the full treatment from the comfort and privacy of your room.

Huge perk and just another thing that puts the Coco Grand a step above.

Easy In-Hotel Laundry

The laundry experience is even great at the Coco Grand. The washing machines have detergent built in, so you don’t have to buy it separately or, even worse, bring it yourself. You just throw in 400 yen and your clothes start spinning.

A Water Bottle and Local Treat, Daily

It’s also worth mentioning that every day, outside your door (if you leave the “do not disturb” sign up) they give you a fresh water bottle and even a local treat when you check in. The water bottle in particular is another one of those small things that puts the Coco Grand above other hotels in the area.

There was one time I wasn’t able to book the Coco Grand because it was Golden Week and they were fully booked, so I had to stay at another hotel nearby. And funnily enough, this is one of the first things I noticed on that first morning. I opened the door, I checked, and I did not have that fresh bottle of water waiting for me.

Key insight: So if you stay at other hotels in the Takasaki Station area, and you’ve stayed at the Coco Grand before, you are going to notice what makes the Coco Grand so great.
